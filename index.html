Geographic

The word geographic (or geographical) relates to geography, which is the study of the Earth’s physical features, climate, populations, and how human activity affects and is affected by them. Essentially, anything described as a "geographic" has to do with location, places, and the spatial relationships between things on Earth.

Geographical Location of Pakistan

Pakistan is located in South Asia, sitting at the crossroads of Central Asia and the Middle East.

Geographic Coordinates:

  • Latitude: 24°35′ N to 37°05′ N
  • Longitude: 61° E to 78° E

Borders and Neighbors

  • East: India (2,912 km border)
  • West and Northwest: Afghanistan (2,430 km border)
  • Southwest: Iran (909 km border)
  • Northeast: China (523 km border)
  • South: Arabian Sea (1,046 km of coastline)
